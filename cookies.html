COOKIES

Cookie Policy.

Last updated 5 October 2026

What are cookies

Cookies are small files a website can ask your browser to store, usually to remember something between visits or to let a third-party service work properly. Some are essential for a site to function; others are used for things like analytics.

Cookies we currently use

The Skate Board doesn't set any first-party cookies of its own. The only third-party embed on the site is the Fillout form widget (used for the "Submit an Event" popup), which may set its own cookies to make the form work — see Fillout's privacy policy for details. Google Fonts, used to load the site's typefaces, doesn't set cookies with the method we use.

Cookies that run only if you accept

These are non-essential, so they are not set unless you accept:

  • Google Analytics — usage statistics, such as pages visited and general location and device information. See Google's cookie information.
  • Microsoft Clarity — heatmaps and session recordings, so we can see how people use the site and fix anything confusing. See Microsoft's privacy statement.
  • Google AdSense — advertising cookies that show ads and measure how they perform. These can be used to personalise the ads you see. See how Google uses advertising cookies.

Google's consent message also stores a cookie recording your choice, so it doesn't ask again on every visit. You can change your ad personalisation at any time in Google's ad settings.

Our own consent banner stores your choice in your browser's local storage, so we don't ask again. You can change it at any time with the "Cookie settings" link in the footer.

How to control cookies

You can block or delete cookies through your browser's settings at any time — this may affect how some embedded features (like the event submission form) work. Most browsers also let you block third-party cookies specifically while still allowing the site itself to function.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page whenever a cookie-setting tool is added, changed or removed.
