COOKIES

Cookie Policy.

Last updated 25 August 2026

What are cookies

Cookies are small files a website can ask your browser to store, usually to remember something between visits or to let a third-party service work properly. Some are essential for a site to function; others are used for things like analytics.

Cookies we currently use

The Skate Board doesn't set any first-party cookies of its own. The only third-party embed on the site is the Fillout form widget (used for the "Submit an Event" popup), which may set its own cookies to make the form work — see Fillout's privacy policy for details. Google Fonts, used to load the site's typefaces, doesn't set cookies with the method we use.

Cookies we plan to add

We're planning to add two tools to understand how the site is used and improve it:

  • Google Analytics — usage statistics (pages visited, general location and device info).
  • Microsoft Clarity — heatmaps and session recordings, so we can see how people actually use the site and fix anything confusing.

Both set non-essential cookies. Before either goes live, we'll add a cookie consent banner so you can accept or decline them, and update this page with the specific cookies each one sets.

How to control cookies

You can block or delete cookies through your browser's settings at any time — this may affect how some embedded features (like the event submission form) work. Most browsers also let you block third-party cookies specifically while still allowing the site itself to function.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page as soon as any new cookie-setting tool goes live on the site.
